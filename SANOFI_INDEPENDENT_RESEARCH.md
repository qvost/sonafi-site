## 4. The Grand Strategic Pivot: Pure-Play Biopharma

### The €16B Opella Carve-Out (Closed April 2025)
- **What happened**: Sanofi sold a **50% controlling stake** in its Consumer Healthcare business, **Opella** (makers of *Doliprane*, *Allegra*, *Dulcolax*), to US private equity firm **Clayton, Dubilier & Rice (CD&R)**.
- **Ownership structure**: CD&R (50.0%), Sanofi (48.2%), French state investment bank Bpifrance (1.8%).
- **Financial windfall**: Sanofi received **~€10 billion in net cash proceeds**.
- **Strategic intent**: Complete liberation from lower-margin consumer OTC products to transform Sanofi into an agile, pure-play innovative biopharmaceutical & immunology powerhouse.

## 5. Flagship Portfolio & Breakthrough Pipeline

### Current Blockbusters
1. **Dupixent (dupilumab)** *(co-developed with Regeneron)*:
   - Sanofi’s crown jewel biotherapeutic (>€11B+ annual sales).
   - Treats Type 2 inflammatory conditions: Atopic Dermatitis, Moderate-to-Severe Asthma, Eosinophilic Esophagitis, Prurigo Nodularis.
   - **Recent Major Catalyst**: Regulatory approval for **COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease)**, unlocking a massive multi-billion-dollar patient population.
2. **Beyfortus (nirsevimab)** *(partnered with AstraZeneca)*:
   - Breakthrough long-acting monoclonal antibody protecting all newborns and infants against Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV) disease. Huge adoption and supply scaling across 19+ countries.
3. **Altuviiio (efanesoctocog alfa)**:
   - Next-generation, first-in-class high-sustained Factor VIII therapy for hemophilia A, offering normal-to-near-normal factor levels for most of the week.
4. **Fluzone High-Dose / Fluarix**:
   - The world’s leading influenza vaccine franchise for seniors aged 65+.
5. **Nexviazyme**:
   - Enzyme replacement therapy for Pompe disease.

### Key Pipeline Candidates in Late Stage
- **Tolebrutinib**: Investigational brain-penetrant oral BTK inhibitor demonstrating groundbreaking Phase 3 efficacy in delaying disability progression in non-relapsing secondary progressive multiple sclerosis (nrSPMS).
- **Duvakitug**: Anti-TL1A antibody co-developed with Teva Pharmaceuticals for Ulcerative Colitis and Crohn’s Disease (positive Phase 2b data presented late 2026).

- **Origin**: Designed in 2022 by **FutureBrand Paris**.
- **The Visual Metaphor**:
  - The logo is rendered in all-lowercase geometric lettering.
  - **Dot 1 (Bottom of the 's')**: Represents the starting point of scientific inquiry—the curiosity to ask *"What if?"*.
  - **Dot 2 (Dot of the 'i')**: Represents the finish line—the *"Eureka!"* moment when breakthrough medicine touches human lives.
